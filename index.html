About this assessment

The Participatory Assessment is a UNHCR community-based protection tool that promotes meaningful engagement with affected populations to better understand their experiences, capacities, priorities and protection concerns. Using a qualitative methodology, the PA gathers and analyses information through Focus Group Discussions conducted with diverse community members, ensuring that different age, gender, diversity and vulnerability perspectives are reflected. The findings support evidence-based protection and programme planning, advocacy, accountability and decision-making, while strengthening the participation of affected communities in shaping responses that impact their lives. In Moldova, the PA is implemented through continuous data collection across both banks of the Nistru River, with significant contributions from members of the AAP Task Force and RCF partners. Findings are consolidated and published in semi-annual reports, accompanied by updates to this dashboard to support ongoing analysis and response planning.
